How the policy is rewarded for driving a nail flush — every reward term, its equation, and where it fires across the ~9-step strike. Weights are the live training values; equations are pinned to the code by a parity test.

$$ r_{\text{app}} = \exp\!\left(-\lVert p_{\text{head}}-p_{\text{nail}}\rVert^2/\sigma^2\right),\ \sigma=0.08 $$

Gaussian that pulls the hammer head toward the nail top. Low weight so hovering near the nail is never a stable optimum. Always on.

nail_depth_deltaprogress+600
$$ r_{\Delta} = \max\!\left(0,\ d - \max_{t'\le t} d_{t'}\right) $$

Progress reward — pays only NEW maximum depth (ratchets on max-so-far), so re-driving already-covered ground earns nothing. 4 mm dead zone at the start.

$$ r_{\text{ip}} = \frac{\max(0,\,v_{\text{axial}})}{v_{\text{exp}}}\cdot \mathbb{1}[\text{first contact}]\cdot \mathbb{1}[\Delta d>\epsilon] $$

Downward impact speed at contact, paid once per fresh contact that actually advances the nail (double-gated). The controllable 'hit hard' lever on a position-only action space.

r_imitante-impact · annealed+0.1

Weak ante-impact tracking prior (A-TRACK arm only): rewards following the scripted strike reference BEFORE contact, then gates off. Annealed to 0 by iter ~250 so the policy ends up free to deviate.
