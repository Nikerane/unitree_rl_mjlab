# Resolution for Design the matched FIC RTT ablation

## Answer

Train FIC-0 and FIC-TT as a paired fixed-gain ablation. Pilot seed 2 for both
arms with identical training and evaluation budgets. Expand both arms together
to seeds 3 and 4 only if both independently pass the same preregistered absolute
task and safety gates. Admit only `model_199.pt`.

Do not require a directional `r_tt` advantage for promotion. Estimate and report
the paired FIC-TT minus FIC-0 effect on command-tracking error, task success,
productive impact, nail impulse, per-joint reaction impulse, action saturation,
joint-limit proximity, and velocity legality. The raw-cost/weighted-return checks
apply only to FIC-TT; FIC-0 must prove the term is absent. The later controller
comparison is FIC-TT versus VIC-TT with the same calibrated `r_tt`.

## Evidence

The primary-source audit shows the Khadiv/Bogdanovic/Righetti paper trained fixed-
gain controllers both without and with `r_tt`. The owner selected the paired
seed-2-first design and explicitly rejected treating FIC-0 as configuration-only.

## Consequences

The Stage-1 plan, launch arrays, manifests, evaluators, and G4/G5 wording must all
carry two arms. If either seed-2 pilot fails, neither arm expands. Historical
Cartesian results remain descriptive and are not part of this causal contrast.
