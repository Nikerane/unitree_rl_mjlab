# Resolution for Use a diagnostic windowed reaction-impulse constraint

## Answer

Use the existing substep-accumulated windowed per-joint reaction impulse for the
first active-CaT validation. Temporarily lower its thresholds enough to create a
diagnostic constraint exposure, compare matched CaT-off and CaT-on policies, and
label the thresholds diagnostic rather than physical safety limits. Defer the
rigorous ballistic estimator.

## Evidence

The owner prioritized VIC progress over a rigorous ballistic estimator and
accepted the windowed diagnostic in the post-meeting discussion summarized by the
[handoff](../../../../results/2026-08-06_PRESENTATION_HANDOFF.md).

## Consequences

The controlled-drop `I_ref` and per-joint CaT thresholds remain different
quantities: the former normalizes delivered nail-axis impulse; the latter bounds
joint reaction impulse.
