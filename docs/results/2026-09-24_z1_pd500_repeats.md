## How to separate the research questions

1. Repeatability: do independent training seeds retain productive strikes and
   measured speed compliance? Compare per-policy counts/tails, not pooled
   episode counts as though they were independent training replications.
2. Explicit speed selection: existing policy-mode/qualification-transfer code
   supports a next bounded phase, but current selector is externally supplied.
   Test post-latch interventions to establish selected goal changes subsequent
   motion, not just predicts achieved speed. Inspect actual speed errors; seed2
   low-goal median bias is about+0.115m/s, so do not assume qualification passed.
3. Learned impedance: sampled fixed controller overwrites gain actions and uses
   its own fixed torque gains. Genuine VIC requires a separately qualified
   sampled-gain path, not simply removing the overwrite. Retain500Hz hold,
   effort/speed limits; test gain authority, bounds, nominal parity and reset.
4. Trajectory family: once those work, compare a shared policy over three
   left/centre/right splines with observable geometry. Current reference is
   geometric guidance and learned timing, not clock-timed trajectory tracking.

These are proposed next phases, not launched or implemented by this submission.
No new hierarchy, trajectory planner, or broad infrastructure is needed now.
