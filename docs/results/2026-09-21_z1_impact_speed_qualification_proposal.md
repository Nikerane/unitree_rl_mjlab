## Proposed bounded sequence and go/no-go rules

1. **Before any GPU job:** complete every unresolved identity above; freeze the **12-output comparator's** reward/curve/reset/safety configuration and checkpoint hash, plus physical interval derivation, event-return coefficient sweep, seeds and budget in a reviewed revision of this record. Repeat the applicable baseline scripts if code/asset state changes. Whole-branch review and a campaign-specific native dry run must be explicitly assessed; software tests alone are not launch approval. No pre-E1 13-output checkpoint is expected.
2. **E1, externally sampled goals:** train the same 13-output policy/controller with the goal present at reset and selector inactive. Evaluate low/middle/high goals on paired initial states/common randomness. Provisional promotion criteria from the approved design: hit rate at least 90% of the matched baseline; median absolute achieved-minus-requested speed error among hits at most 10% of the frozen interval width; paired high-minus-low achieved speed positive with 95% confidence interval excluding zero. Report coverage/misses and speed tails alongside the conditional estimate. Tracking RMS may degrade no more than 10% relative to a nonzero matched baseline, with absolute millimetres also reported. Keep safety caps/dose fixed and report exceedance frequencies, CaT and maximum loads; no hardware-safety inference.
3. **E2 only after E1 passes:** use **E1's qualified external-mode 13-output checkpoint** for the explicit transfer, initializing the previously inactive selector row/std with a fresh optimizer while preserving trained controller/critic/normalizers. The 12-output comparator checkpoint is never loaded into this actor. Policy-selected goals then condition motion only from the second decision. Run normal self-selected evaluation and separately labeled post-latch goal interventions; inspect command/gain changes, selected-versus-achieved speed, saturation, actual delivered impulse, tracking, hit rate and joint loads.
4. **Later, only after the one-curve gates:** three existing curves, then matched-seed comparison against the unchanged baseline with red-reference video and white-background trajectory/speed plots. A first training seed is a feasibility pilot, not a scientific conclusion. If E1 or E2 responsiveness fails, **stop**; do not silently switch to a spline planner, add a timing constraint/dense reward, mix external goals indefinitely, enlarge the budget, or declare a best impedance setting.

**Current decision: NO-GO for GPU qualification.** The matched **12-output comparator** choice/checkpoint, reset-library hash, physical interval, event-return coefficient sweep, training/evaluation seeds and budget are not frozen. The smallest proposed next action is matched-comparator evidence/replay and review of this completed record—not an E1 launch and not a search for a pre-existing 13-output checkpoint.
