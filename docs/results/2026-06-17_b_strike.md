# b_strike — honest-scale single strike (2026-06-17)

**Run:** Vega array `36517248_[0-2]` · 3 seeds × 500 iters · A-BASE 7-term reward · all COMPLETED ~7 min/A100.
**One-line:** Raising `delta_pos_scale` to an honest value turns the gentle V1 "strike" into a **real single ballistic strike** (~1.2 m/s, 100% success, press still excluded) — **but** the trained policy now drives joints past the real Z1 velocity limit (3.1415 rad/s; worst-case 4.3–4.65). The strike is *real but not yet honest at the hardware velocity limit.*

---

## 1. Context

V1 (see `../VEGA_TRAINING_PLAN.md` → "V1 — Results") established that the slow-press exploit dissolves under training and the policy "strikes." But the V1 strike was a *gentle ~0.45 m/s, ~3-contact-step drive* — and a follow-up probe showed that gentleness was an **artifact of `delta_pos_scale=0.05`**, not a hardware limit (head speed ≈ 0.18·`delta_pos_scale`/dt). The single-strike redesign asked: at an honest action scale, does a real impact emerge, does press stay excluded, and do joint speeds stay within the real Z1 limit?

## 2. What changed (vs V1 / A-BASE)

Three commits on `hammer-z1` (`../archive/2026-06-17-z1-single-strike-fix.md`):
- `7dca1bc` — **clamp** nail depth to its physical range `[0, NAIL_GOAL_DEPTH]` at the reward/termination source (kills the depth-overshoot ratchet).
- `94e4249` — **`delta_pos_scale` 0.05 → 0.15** (real strike), and **no `max_dq` rail** (Option A: trust the open-loop "self-limiting at 2.41 rad/s < 3.1415 rad/s" probe finding).
- `4eece58` — liveness-sweep test hygiene (sparse `completion` exempt).

Reward function itself unchanged from A-BASE (augment-not-replace): `approach 0.1, nail_driven 2.0, nail_depth_delta 600, impact_progress 8, completion 100, action_rate −0.01, joint_pos_limits −10`.

## 3. Method

```bash
# train (Vega): 3 seeds, A-BASE reward, new config
ITERS=500 RUN=b_strike TASK=Unitree-Z1-Hammer sbatch --array=0-2 scripts/slurm/train_array.sbatch

# verify (local, CPU), per seed:
python scripts/diag_policy_trace.py  --task Unitree-Z1-Hammer --ckpt <model_499.pt> --num-envs 64 --nsteps 80 --device cpu
python scripts/diag_strike_probe.py  --mode press_basin --task Unitree-Z1-Hammer --ckpt <model_499.pt> --device cpu
```
`diag_policy_trace.py` was extended to report **peak arm joint speed (rad/s)** with a `>3.1415` over-limit flag — the safety measurand for this redesign, read straight from `robot.data.joint_vel` (not a finite difference).

## 4. Results

| metric | V1 (delta=0.05) | b_strike (delta=0.15) |
|---|---|---|
| success rate | 100% | **100%** (all seeds) |
| episode length (control steps) | ~8.6 | **~4.0** |
| contacts / episode | ~3 (drive) | **~1 (single ballistic strike)** |
| peak impact speed @ contact | ~0.45 m/s | **~1.2 m/s** (2.7×) |
| peak arm joint speed (mean / **worst**) | — | **2.7–3.7 / 4.3–4.65 rad/s** |
| press exploit | excluded | **excluded** (lift-and-strike) |

Per seed (`model_499`, 64 envs × 80 steps):

| seed | success | ep len | contacts/ep | impact @ contact | joint speed mean / **worst** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100% | 3.85 | 0.96 | 1.235 m/s | 2.67 / **4.29** |
| 1 | 100% | 4.56 | 0.99 | 1.254 m/s | 3.75 / **4.65** |
| 2 | 100% | 3.91 | 0.94 | 1.186 m/s | 2.74 / **4.51** |

Contact-count histograms are dominated by **1 contact/episode** (e.g. seed 0: `0:49  1:1258`) — a genuine single strike, not V1's multi-step drive. Per-term rewards at iter 499 match V1's shape (`completion 0.10`, `impact_progress ~0.019`, `nail_depth_delta ~0.017`, `joint_pos_limits ≈ 0`).

**Press-basin probe (seed 0):** the policy was placed in the press basin (in contact, v≈0, ~7 mm depth) then handed control. Within one step it **lifted 49.8 mm out and broke contact** (−2.8 m/s, moving up) rather than pushing → VERDICT **LIFT-AND-STRIKE**. Press stays excluded at the higher scale.

## 5. Conclusion (two-sided)

**Worked:** The redesign delivered exactly the intended strike character — a single ballistic contact at ~1.2 m/s (2.7× V1), 100% success, episodes halved to ~4 control steps, press still excluded. The original two questions ("why no strike?" / "how to avoid press?") are answered: the gentle strike was a scale artifact, and press is a non-issue.

**Broke (the key finding): Option A ("self-limiting, no rail") is FALSIFIED for the closed-loop policy.** The open-loop straight-down ceiling at delta=0.15 is 2.41 rad/s (< 3.1415), but the *trained* policy amplifies via windup / off-axis Jacobian to **4.3–4.65 rad/s worst-case (≈1.9× the open-loop ceiling)** on every seed, and seed 1's *mean* (3.75) already exceeds the limit. So the 1.2 m/s strike partly relies on joint speeds the real Z1 cannot reach. `joint_pos_limits ≈ 0` confirms the arm is not at *position* limits; MuJoCo enforces no *velocity* limit, so nothing bounds the overshoot.

This is itself a clean Phase-0 diagnostic result: **on a position-only fixed-PD action space, the binding constraint for a hard strike is joint velocity** — which directly motivates the thesis's variable-impedance + explicit-constraint direction.

## 6. Decision / next — how to bound joint velocity

Researched in depth → **`../research/reward-design/CONSTRAINED_RL_LANDSCAPE.md`** (workflow `wf_aa165e9a-cb9` + the CaT paper). Headline conclusions:

- The overshoot is **chain-coupled / off-axis windup** (momentum delivered through the linkage), so no single-joint or steady-state fix is a hard worst-case bound; an honest hard bound needs a plant model **+** action projection, and even then a tail leaks (as on real hardware).
- **Velocity is the hardware-honesty target, not the thesis target** — the thesis constrains per-joint *impulse* `Λ_j = Σ|qfrc_constraint_j|·h`. Bound velocity as a hardware guard, but **log `Λ_j` in parallel** so the rehearsal is on the correct signal.
- **CaT (Constraints as Terminations)** is the preferred low-infra delivery for the soft constraint (scale-free, manager-based → ports to mjlab, same machinery reused for the G1 impulse constraint) — see `[[cat-constraints-as-terminations]]`.

**Recommended next step — the cheap, decisive experiment first:** retrain with an honest velocity bound (simplest: `delta_pos_scale` 0.15→0.10, and/or a CaT velocity constraint at π) to answer **"does the strike survive an honest velocity limit?"** If it survives → honest baseline, then decide on a tighter bound. If it dies → that *is* the Phase-0 finding ("position-only fixed-PD striking is velocity-limited"), which motivates the thesis's variable-impedance move; record and pivot to G1.

> **RESOLVED → `2026-06-17_velocity_bound_ablation.md`.** Ran the A1–A4 ablation (delta→0.10 / penalty / CaT / DcMotor). The strike *survives* every bound (100%), but **none gets worst-case under π** — the residual is chain-coupled. Decision: adopt **CaT** as the mechanism, carry to the G1; a targeted CaT-knob ablation (hard p_max + substep) is the next experiment.
